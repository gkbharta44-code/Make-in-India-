Project Make in India 🇮🇳

Executive Summary (aligned with the Delper Group Executive Summary)

  • Total investment: $56.00 Trillion, financed by 10-year bonds at 9% interest. $48.88 Trillion is outstanding at issue in Year 18.
  • Factories: Approximately 560,000 dark factories on 1-acre coastal sites
  • Power: 840 GW of dedicated zero-carbon nuclear baseload
  • Market share: 28% of world physical manufacturing value-add
  • Profit at full scale: $1.27 Trillion a year after the 20% conversion cost and 17% tax (base case); $0.52 Trillion a year (demand-gated case)
  • Repayment: From the shared net profit pool, after Iron Nexus. Bharat is cleared in Year 27.
Alignment changes: the repayment year is Year 27 (not Year 20). Bharat is issued at the end of Year 18 (not Year 15). All Bharat profit goes to the shared pool, so the 60% reserve and 40% pool split is removed. The $4.71 Trillion figure is revenue-like and is converted to profit in the table below. The $16.52 Trillion capitalised-interest and "15 years ahead" claims are removed because the Group summary does not contain them.

1. Overview

  1. What it is: A coastal, software-defined manufacturing matrix in India, with dark factories that run without on-site staff.
  2. Scale: About 560,000 factories along a 7,500 km coastal corridor.
  3. Output target: 28% of world physical manufacturing value-add.
  4. Role in the programme: One of two projects, with Project Iron Nexus ($46.00T), making up the $102.00T programme.

2. Rollout

[Year 15]      Project Iron Nexus operative (Bharat's build starts after this)
[Years 16-18]  Set-up; no Bharat profit yet. Bharat bonds issued at end of Year 18.
[Years 19-22]  Bharat profit $0.11T a year (after 20% conversion cost and 17% tax)
[Years 23-26]  Bharat profit $0.68T a year
[Year 27+]     Bharat profit $1.27T a year at full scale; bonds cleared; distributions begin

Factory milestones follow the Group deployment curve: 50,000 factories engaged in Phase 2, 300,000 active in Phase 3, and 560,000 at full scale in Phase 4. On the demand-gated case, factories are limited to 228,500 from Year 14 (40.8% of the 560,000 plan).

3. Profit and Cash Allocation

  • Profit basis: The $4.71 Trillion figure is a revenue-like estimate of 28% of world manufacturing value-add at 2024 levels. A 20% conversion cost and the 17% tax are applied before it reaches the pool, giving $1.54 Trillion pre-tax and $1.27 Trillion after tax at full scale.
  • Shared pool: All Make in India profit goes into the shared net profit pool. Repayment is Iron Nexus first and then Bharat.
  • After clearance: Once both bond series are cleared in Year 27, 100% of net profit goes to GK Bharta.

6. Human Capital and Security

  • Engineers: 560,000 Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) in total at full scale, organised as 7 hubs of 80,000 FDEs each.
  • Training: A 12-month, online, credit-bearing Post-Graduate Advanced Diploma with UVCE and Bangalore University. Because training is online, no physical training hubs are required.
  • Maritime defence: The Indian Navy's Maritime Domain Awareness bubble, with BrahMos batteries covering mid-tier lanes.
  • Perimeter security: CISF, with anti-drone interceptor domes and air-gapped private 5G cores.

7. Risks

  • Demand: The Group summary says demand is the binding limit from Year 10. Reaching 560,000 factories needs about $4.71T of demand a year, against 35% of adults buying assumed in the model. Tariffs and domestic competition are not modelled.
  • Interest exposure: Bharat carries 9% interest. On $48.88T outstanding at issue, that is about $4.40T a year from Year 20, before any principal is repaid. The Group summary gives $4.80T, which implies a balance of about $53.36T (its own Year 18 figure in the Make in India table). This needs to be resolved by the Group.
  • Profit basis: The $4.71T figure is revenue-like. If the conversion cost or tax changes, the after-tax profit changes with it.
