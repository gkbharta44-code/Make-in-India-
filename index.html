Project Make in India 🇮🇳

Executive Summary

Key Figures at a Glance:
  • Total Investment: $56.00 Trillion, financed by 10-year bonds at 9% interest
  • Factories: Approximately 560,000 dark factories on 1-acre coastal sites
  • Power: 840 GW of dedicated zero-carbon nuclear baseload
  • Market Share: 28% of world physical manufacturing value-add
  • Profit at Full Scale: About $4.71 Trillion per year after tax (2024 world levels)
  • Repayment: From pooled net profit, after Iron Nexus; cleared in Year 20

1. Overview

  1. What it is: A coastal, software-defined manufacturing matrix in India, with dark factories that run without on-site staff.
  2. Scale: 560,000 factories along a 7,500 km coastal corridor.
  3. Output target: 28% of world physical manufacturing value-add.
  4. Role in the programme: One of two projects, with Project Iron Nexus ($40.00T), making up the $96.00T programme.

2. Rollout Timeline

Year 15: Project Iron Nexus operative (pre-condition for Make in India 🇮🇳)
Years 16–18 (Phase 1): Set-up phase; no profit generated.
Years 19–22 (Phase 2): Years 4–7 of build. First 50,000 factories engaged; profit of $0.42T per year.
Years 23–26 (Phase 3): Years 8–11 of build. 300,000 factories active; profit of $2.52T per year.
Year 27+ (Phase 4): Years 12+ of build. 560,000 factories and 840 GW at full scale; profit of $4.71T per year.

Note: Make in India 🇮🇳's build starts after Iron Nexus is operative in Year 15. Phase timings are shown relative to that start.

3. Profit and Cash Allocation

  • Profit to pool: Make in India 🇮🇳's after-tax profit goes into the shared net profit pool.
  • Reserve: 60% of Make in India 🇮🇳 profit is held as corporate reserve until Make in India 🇮🇳's bonds are cleared.
  • Debt service: 40% of Make in India 🇮🇳 profit goes to the pool, which repays Iron Nexus first and then Make in India 🇮🇳.
  • After clearance: From Year 21, 100% of net profit goes to GK Bharta.

6. Human Capital and Security

  • Engineers: 560,000 Forward Deployed Engineers, with seven per hub for 24/7 monitoring across three 8-hour shifts.
  • Training: A 12-month, online, credit-bearing Post-Graduate Advanced Diploma with UVCE and Bangalore University, scaling to 2,147 physical hubs by Year 3.
  • Maritime defence: The Indian Navy's Maritime Domain Awareness bubble, with BrahMos batteries covering mid-tier lanes.
  • Perimeter security: CISF, with anti-drone interceptor domes and air-gapped private 5G cores.

7. Risks and Open Items

  • Engineer count discrepancy: Seven FDEs per hub across 560,000 hubs implies about 3.9 million engineers required, not the stated 560,000. This figure requires confirmation.
  • Revenue basis: The 28% target is a value-add figure. Converting it to gross revenue requires an input-cost ratio not provided in the files.
  • Timing data gaps: Year-by-year profit projections are only provided for Phase 2. Phases 3 and 4 are listed only as flat, steady levels.
  • Training bonds: The ₹20.0 crore per-hub refund exposure requires a legal review to separate liability from top-line tuition revenue.
  • Interest exposure: The $56.00T principal carries a 9% annual interest rate, representing a $5.04T annual obligation before capitalization structures are applied.
